ADD THE OFFICIAL LOGO
=====================

Save your two logo files into THIS folder (darsh-innovations/public/):

    logo-full.svg    the lockup  -> swirl mark + "Darsh Innovations" text
    logo-mark.svg    the mark    -> the swirl on its own, no text

SVG is the one to ask your designer for. The mark has gradients and a fine
star, and it is drawn everywhere from a 28px favicon to a 400px hero — only a
vector stays crisp across that range, and it weighs a few KB instead of a few
hundred.

If you only have a raster file, a transparent PNG works. Export the mark at
1024x1024 and the lockup at 2400px wide, both with a transparent background
(not the grey checkerboard - that is just how transparency is *displayed*), and
name them logo-full.png / logo-mark.png.

Either way, update the two paths in src/siteConfig.js to match the extension
you saved:

    logoFull: '/logo-full.svg',
    logoMark: '/logo-mark.svg',

Until those files exist, the site draws the vector stand-in in public/
logo-mark.svg plus a typeset "Darsh Innovations", so nothing ever shows as a
broken image.
